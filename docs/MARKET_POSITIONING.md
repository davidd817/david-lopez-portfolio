# Market Positioning Notes

## Best-fit roles

- Materials Characterization Scientist
- Metrology Scientist
- R&D Scientist / Research Scientist
- Nanomaterials Scientist
- Application Scientist — AFM, microscopy, surface characterization
- Scientific Instrumentation Specialist
- Research Engineer
- Laboratory Scientist — Advanced Characterization

## Hybrid roles

- Scientific Data Analyst
- R&D Data Analyst
- Materials Data Analyst
- Scientific Computing Specialist
- AI for Science / Materials Informatics — junior or hybrid profile
- Data Scientist — Materials / Biotech / R&D contexts

## Secondary roles

- Data Analyst
- Junior Data Scientist
- Business Intelligence Analyst
- Machine Learning Analyst
- Analytics Consultant

These can be targeted, but they should not be the leading identity because they make the profile look more generic.

## Market rationale

The market increasingly rewards hybrid professionals who combine domain expertise, analytical thinking, data fluency and technology literacy. For David, the strongest differentiator is not generic analytics, but applying reproducible data workflows inside real scientific and R&D contexts.
